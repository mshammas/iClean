# iClean Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

iClean is an iPhone app that helps you free up storage by finding duplicate,
blurry, screenshot, and large-video items in your photo library so you can
review and delete the ones you don't want.

## The short version

**iClean collects no personal data, and nothing about you or your photos ever
leaves your device.** There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no
third-party tracking. iClean has no network code at all.

## What iClean accesses, and why

- **Your photo library.** With your permission, iClean reads your photos and
  videos *on your device* to analyse them (detecting duplicates, blur,
  screenshots, and large videos). This analysis happens entirely on your
  iPhone. Photos are never uploaded, copied off the device, or shared.
- **Deleting items.** When you review the results and confirm, iClean asks iOS
  to move the items you chose into the system **Recently Deleted** album, where
  they remain recoverable for 30 days. iClean never deletes anything without
  your explicit confirmation, and always keeps at least one copy of every
  duplicate group.

## Data iClean stores on your device

To make repeat scans faster, iClean keeps a small cache on your device (in the
app's private storage) recording what it has already analysed. This cache stays
on your iPhone, is excluded from device backups, and never leaves the device.
You can clear it at any time from the app's home screen ("Clear Saved Data").

iClean also stores a single on/off flag recording whether you have completed
the welcome screen. That is the only preference it saves.

## Data iClean does not collect

iClean does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information,
usage data, location, contacts, identifiers, or photo content. It contains no
advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Children's privacy

iClean does not collect data from anyone, including children.
