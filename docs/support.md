# iClean Support

iClean helps you free up space on your iPhone by finding duplicate, blurry,
screenshot, and large video files so you can review and delete the ones you
don't want.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is anything uploaded to the internet?**
No. iClean checks your photos entirely on your iPhone. Nothing about you or your
photos ever leaves the device. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy).

**Will I lose photos by accident?**
iClean never deletes anything on its own. You review every item and confirm
before anything is removed. Deleted items go to your **Recently Deleted** album
in the Photos app, where they stay recoverable for 30 days.

**How do I get a deleted photo back?**
Open the Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted, select the photo, and tap
Recover. This works for 30 days after deletion.

**Does deleting also free up iCloud space?**
Yes. Because your photos sync with iCloud, deleting them on your iPhone clears
the space in iCloud too.

**Why didn't iClean find any duplicates / blurry photos?**
That usually means your library is already tidy in that category. iClean is
deliberately cautious — it only flags photos it is confident about, so it errs
toward suggesting fewer rather than risking a photo you want.

**Which iPhones are supported?**
iClean requires iOS 17 or later.
